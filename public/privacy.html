Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

In short: we collect only what's needed to sign you in, keep track of your songs, and read your scores. Page images are sent to our AI provider to be read and are not kept on our servers. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

Who we are

Score to Sound (scoretosound.com) is operated by [OPERATOR NAME]. If you have a question about your information, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign in with Google, also your name and your Google account ID, which Google shares with us when you choose to sign in that way.
  • Your songs: how many AI songs you have and a record of each time songs are added, used or refunded.
  • AI readings: for each score you read with AI, the file name, the number of pages, a fingerprint of the file (a code that identifies the file without containing it), and the notes and lyrics read from each page. We keep these so that opening the same score again is free. We also record which AI model read each page and how much processing it used.
  • Sign-in and security: a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in, single-use sign-in links, and short-lived records of email addresses and internet (IP) addresses used to prevent abuse, such as too many sign-in emails or free trials.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (for example, the time of a request and any errors) to keep the service running.

What stays on your device

Scores you open from MusicXML files are processed in your browser and are not sent to us. The app also remembers the last score you opened, and your key, tempo and part choices, in your browser's storage on your own device.

Page images

When you read a PDF or photos with AI, each page is turned into an image in your browser and sent to us, and we pass it to Anthropic, our AI provider, to read the notes. We don't store the page images or your original files on our servers. Anthropic processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure
  • To read your scores and let you open them again for free
  • To keep track of your free trial and the songs you've bought
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service running
  • To send sign-in emails. We don't send marketing emails unless you've agreed to them.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare: hosts the app and stores our database.
  • Anthropic: reads page images with AI.
  • Resend: sends sign-in emails.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • A payment processor, when paid plans launch, to handle payments. We won't see or store your full card number.

These providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

Cookies

We use one cookie, which keeps you signed in. It's needed for the service to work. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details, songs and readings while your account exists, so that opening a score again stays free. Sign-in links expire after 20 minutes, and sign-in sessions after 30 days. Abuse-prevention records are kept only briefly. If you delete your account, we delete your information, except where we must keep some records (for example, payment records required for tax purposes).

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to delete your account, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL]. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under privacy law, including the right to complain to your local privacy regulator.

Children

Score to Sound isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Connections to Score to Sound are encrypted. Sign-in sessions are stored only as scrambled codes, and our AI provider key never reaches your browser. No service can promise perfect security, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app or by email.
